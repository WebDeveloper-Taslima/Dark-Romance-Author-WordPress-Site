# Christiana Ciocan - Website Owner Handoff Guide
### How to Manage Your Dark Romance WordPress & WooCommerce Portal

Welcome to your new author platform! This manual was prepared specifically so you can effortlessly manage, update, and sell your books with complete independence.

---

## 1. How to Add a New Book in WooCommerce

1. Log into your WordPress Dashboard (`yoursite.com/wp-admin`).
2. In the left menu, hover over **Products** and click **Add New**.
3. **Product Name**: Enter the novel title (e.g., *The Devil's Rose*).
4. **Description**: Paste your extended book description, reviews, and trigger warnings.
5. **Product Data Dropdown**: Select **Variable product** (allows offering both eBook and Paperback).
6. **Attributes Tab**:
   - Select `Custom product attribute` → click **Add**.
   - Name: `Format`
   - Values: `eBook | Paperback | Signed Hardcover` (separated by the pipe symbol `|`).
   - Check the box: **"Used for variations"** → click **Save attributes**.
7. **Variations Tab**:
   - Click **Generate variations** (or Add variation manually).
   - For **eBook**: Check `Virtual` and `Downloadable`. Set your price (e.g. `$5.99`), and upload your `.epub` / `.pdf` files.
   - For **Paperback**: Leave unchecked. Set your price (e.g. `$19.99`), add stock quantity, and shipping dimensions.
8. **Short Description** (at bottom): Paste the dramatic teaser quote or book hook.
9. **Product Image** (right sidebar): Upload the high-resolution front cover graphic.
10. Click **Publish**. Your novel is now live and ready to take orders!

## 2. How to Publish a Writing Teaser or Blog Post

1. Go to **Posts > Add New**.
2. **Title**: Enter your update title (e.g., *Behind the Shadows: Deleted Ballroom Scene*).
3. **Content**: Type or paste your story teaser, character playlist, or update.
4. **Categories**: Check or add a category on the right sidebar (e.g., *Deleted Scenes*, *Cover Reveals*, *Writing News*).
5. **Featured Image**: Set a dramatic moody background or character art.
6. Click **Publish**. It will automatically appear on your blog page.

---

## 3. How to Create Discount Coupons (e.g. 20% Off Launch Sale)

1. In the dashboard, click **Marketing > Coupons**.
2. Click **Add Coupon**.
3. **Coupon Code**: Type the promo word readers will enter at checkout (e.g. `DARKROMANCE20`).
4. **Coupon Data**:
   - **Discount Type**: Select `Percentage discount` (or `Fixed cart discount`).
   - **Coupon Amount**: Enter `20` (for 20% off).
   - **Coupon Expiry Date**: Set the deadline if you want it to expire automatically.
5. **Usage Restriction** (optional): Set a minimum spend or limit to specific books.
6. Click **Publish**. Share the code on your TikTok, Instagram, or newsletter!

---

## 4. How to Process Orders & Track Sales

1. Navigate to **WooCommerce > Orders**.
2. You will see all customer purchases listed:
   - Orders with only eBooks will automatically be marked **Completed** (the customer has already received their download links by email).
   - Orders containing Paperbacks will appear as **Processing**.
3. Click on the order to view the reader's shipping address.
4. Once you package and mail the signed book, add the postal tracking number and change the order status to **Completed**.
5. The reader will automatically receive a dispatch notification email!

---

## 5. How to Edit Pages with Elementor

1. Visit any page on your website while logged into WordPress.
2. Click the top admin bar button: **"Edit with Elementor"**.
3. Click on any text, heading, or button to change its wording or link in the left panel.
4. Click the green **Update** button at the bottom left to save your changes instantly.
